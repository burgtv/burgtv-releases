# BurgTV

BurgTV is a media player for your own IPTV sources: M3U playlists, Xtream Codes accounts, Jellyfin servers and
Stalker/MAG portals. It runs on Fire TV, Google TV, Android TV, Android phones and tablets, iPhone, iPad, Macs with
Apple silicon and Apple Vision Pro.

**BurgTV does not provide, sell or host any channels or content.** You add your own legitimate source.

## Download

- **Fire TV, Google TV, Android TV and Android phones:** https://download.burgtv.com
- **iPhone, iPad, Mac (Apple silicon) and Apple Vision Pro:** [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6761636987)

The TV app updates itself: when a new version is out, it asks you to confirm.

## What it does

- TV guide (EPG) as a full grid, from Xtream Codes, `url-tvg` or extra XMLTV sources
- Timeshift (pause and rewind live TV), catch-up when your source offers an archive, recordings on the TV
- Multi-View: up to 4 channels on one screen (TV app)
- Profiles, a Kids profile and parental controls with PIN, age limits, viewing hours and daily limit
- Skip intro and recap, actor pages, link the TV from your phone (encrypted), subtitles from a file or an address
- 21 app languages, no ads

Full feature list, device by device: https://burgtv.com/en/features/
